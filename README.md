# The impact of data analytics gamified business simulation courses on motivation and cognitive engagement in retail management

Based on the fact that the completion rate of traditional courses is only 72%, through the preliminary questionnaire survey (N=100), the core needs of "timely feedback" and "scenario-based tasks" were identified by building student portraits. The incentive system was constructed by applying octagonal behavior analysis and a complete PRD demand document was written, and the prototype of high-fidelity gamified learning products in the field of retail management was designed by Axure. Collaborate with 5 education team members to develop a business game simulation course, using an agile iterative model to collect feedback every two weeks to optimize the game design. The data of 30 volunteers were analyzed quantitatively and qualitatively by using mixed methods like Li Kert scale questionnaire and in-depth interview. SPSS and nvivo were used to analyze 600+ pieces of user learning behavior data and 80+ pieces of user feedback. Tableau was used to visualize the data and make multi-type charts and main kanliston. The results showed a 24% increase in motivation and cognitive engagement, a 16% increase in user retention, and a 17% increase in course completion.
